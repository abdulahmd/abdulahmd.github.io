Crazyflie deployment pod for the Clearpath Jackal

CIrENs Lab, in progress

A ground rover that carries and launches a small drone swarm. Servo-lidded pods on a parametric frame, driven by an ESP32 off the Jackal's power rail.

  • Parametric OpenSCAD frame: 450 × 323 mm ring, 140 mm pods with optical-flow sensor openings
  • ESP32 with MG90S servos and transistor-switched status LEDs, buck-converted from the Jackal's 12 V rail
  • PTP time sync (linuxptp) between an Ouster LiDAR and the Jackal for aligned scans
