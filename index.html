H-SLAM: heterogeneous air-ground SLAM

A Clearpath Jackal with a LiDAR and a depth camera carries three Crazyflie 2.0 drones fitted with cameras. The rover covers the area along a space-filling curve, with move_base planning each leg using RRT. Wherever it can't drive, it launches a drone on a reconnaissance path to map that region from above. ORB-SLAM fuses both views into one map, and a VLM on the host computer labels the objects in it. Once mapping is done, you can prompt the team to go to any of them.

ROS, move_base, RRT, ORB-SLAM, Clearpath Jackal, Crazyflie 2.0, VLM

Lunabotics: mapping and navigation

I lead mapping and navigation for Comet Aerobotics in the NASA Lunabotics challenge. The rover builds its map with RTAB-Map and plans around craters and boulders with Nav2 and an RRT planner. Then it loops between the excavation zone and the construction zone, digging regolith and depositing it into a berm.

ROS 2, Nav2, RTAB-Map, RRT, micro-ROS, ESP32, CAN
